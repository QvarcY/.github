# Code of Conduct

Projects maintained under the **QvarcY** account should be useful places to build, learn, report problems, and collaborate.

## Expected behavior

Please:

- be respectful and constructive
- discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people
- assume good intent while still being clear about technical disagreements
- keep feedback relevant to the project
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, spam, and other behavior that makes participation unsafe or needlessly hostile are not acceptable.

## Moderation

Content that violates these expectations may be edited, hidden, or removed. Repeated or serious violations may result in restricted participation.

Repository-specific conduct rules override this default file.
